Thank you for using Choosr. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the app. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

01 Who We Are

Choosr is an independent app developed by a solo developer. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out using the contact details at the bottom of this page.

02 Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to operate the app:

  • Device Identifier — A unique device code is collected to enable core app functionality, such as saving your preferences and providing a consistent experience across sessions.

We do not collect your name, email address, location, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it.

03 How We Use Your Information

The device identifier is used solely to:

  • Provide and maintain app functionality
  • Remember your in-app choices and preferences
  • Improve the stability and performance of the app

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, or any purpose beyond what is described above.

04 Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your information with third parties, except in these limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers — Trusted third-party services (such as analytics or crash reporting) may process device identifiers on our behalf. They are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only as directed.
  • Legal Requirements — We may disclose information if required by law or in response to a valid legal request.

05 Data Retention

We retain device identifiers only for as long as necessary to provide the service. If you delete the app, locally stored data is removed from your device. Any server-side data will be deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period after app deletion.

06 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Know what data we hold about your device
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of any non-essential data collection

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

07 Children's Privacy

Choosr is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

08 Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

09 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
